How the University Recovery Process Works

Designed with student safety, privacy protection, and automated match detection.

1️⃣

Submit a Report

File a simple description with item specifications, campus location, color, and photos. You will receive a unique Report ID.

2️⃣

Automated Matching

Our smart scoring algorithm compares category, dates, keywords, and campus locations to suggest possible matches instantly.

3️⃣

Secure Claim & Return

Submit a verified ownership claim with distinctive proof (ID, contents, serials). Campus staff verifies and coordinates the handoff.

About Student Lost & Found

Student Lost & Found is the centralized recovery directory serving our university campus. Whether you misplaced your AirPods at the student center, dropped your dorm room keys in the gym, or discovered a binder in a lecture hall, our platform coordinates safe reunions.

Privacy Guarantee: Student contact info (phone numbers and student emails) is shielded from public browsing to prevent spam or unauthorized contact. Communications and claims are verified through our administrative recovery team.
