Staff Standard Operating Procedures

  • Incoming Found Items: Verify item has a physical property tag matching its FOUND-XXXXXX Report ID.
  • Reviewing Claims: Check whether claimant provided distinct proof (e.g. university ID match, phone passcodes, unique internal marks).
  • Physical Return: Require claimant to sign physical custody receipt at Campus Center Suite 104 and set item status to Returned.
  • Moderation: Delete spam reports or test submissions using the Delete action in All Reports.
